# Memory and privacy

Leonardo Tales Constitution v1.1.1 governs internal project decisions.

Keep synthetic Task records under the control of the operator in a private local directory. The host retains operation attribution, policy observations, review and access records, and fixture evidence. Gateway persists approval, allowance, execution, and audit state. Use those records to inspect a Task and recover its history.

Evidence supports continuity and verification. Authorization comes from the current host-bound role, Task scope, exact approval and Gateway admission. Keep credentials, real personal information, internal correspondence and unrelated workspace data out of Task input, memory, and evidence. Use purpose-made synthetic summaries for public review. Operators remain responsible for securing and retaining their Task directories.
